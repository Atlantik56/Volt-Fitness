# VOLT Swim Design Contract v1.0

## Product position

VOLT Swim is a major training section inside VOLT. The interface must always preserve the identity of the parent product.

## Visual direction

Core mood:

> Спокойствие. Вода. Технологичность. Премиальность.

### Background

- Use a dark swimming-pool environment with lane markings, depth and underwater light.
- Background must remain visible but never reduce text readability.
- Apply dark overlays and local gradients behind dense content.
- Never add people, swimmers, silhouettes or body parts.

### Color system

- Base: deep navy / near-black blue.
- Primary accent: electric blue.
- Secondary accent: cyan / turquoise.
- Success: restrained green only for semantic states.
- Warning/error colors are semantic and must not become decorative glow.

### Glass surfaces

- Rounded translucent cards.
- Subtle blur and inner highlight.
- Thin cool border.
- Soft shadow; no heavy black floating shadows.
- Glow is reserved for active, selected or primary-action states.

### Typography

- Clear modern sans-serif.
- Strong hierarchy: page title, hero value, section title, body, metadata.
- Metrics use large numerals with compact units.
- Small secondary text must keep sufficient contrast.

### Navigation

Global VOLT navigation remains visible on desktop.

Inside Swim use contextual destinations:

- Главная
- План тренировок
- История
- Аналитика
- Рекорды
- AI Coach

Do not create a second independent app shell or a competing VOLT Swim logo hierarchy.

### Layout

- Desktop is the visual source of truth.
- Use a stable content grid and consistent card rhythm.
- Each page should have one dominant primary block.
- Avoid dashboards filled with equally weighted cards.
- Charts must be clean and free of decorative artifacts or unexplained lines.

## Interaction principles

- The primary action must be obvious.
- Garmin records the workout; VOLT plans, tracks state, confirms and analyses.
- During swimming, no manual completion of sets is required.
- History answers: **«Что я делал?»**
- Analytics answers: **«Почему показатели меняются и что делать дальше?»**

## Prohibited

- People or swimmer photography.
- Random neon decoration.
- Excessive chart gridlines and visual artifacts.
- Fake metrics or fabricated AI advice.
- Duplicate navigation systems.
- Mobile mockups treated as a separate visual product.
